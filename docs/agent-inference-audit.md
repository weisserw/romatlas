# Autonomous structural inference

The local Super Off Road investigation exposed a gap between the existing static
evidence policy and the agent's stopping decisions. The prompts explicitly allowed
static inference, but repeatedly emphasized insufficient patterns, exhausted methods
and unchanged retries. They did not define how to combine independent constraints
or correct a previous rejection without obtaining new runtime observations.

The handoffs provide concrete evidence of the resulting reasoning:

- An unresolved mixed interval was treated as one allocation question. Failure to
  establish its entire container persisted as a rejection of a later uniform suffix.
- A test of one exact numerical generator became a reason to reject the broader
  quantized representation. The reported discrepancies were in already classified
  words, yet this acceptance inconsistency was not examined.
- Earlier conclusions were repeatedly cited as exhausted methods. The distinction
  between a measured counterexample, missing evidence and a sufficiency judgment
  was lost in compact handoffs.
- The coordinator checked preservation and accepted terminal self-assessments,
  without examining the decisive inference behind the strongest residual barriers.

These observations support a prompt diagnosis; they do not isolate prompt wording
as the sole cause of model behavior. The previous external suggestions are candidate
inferences, not automatic classification precedents or proof that every residual
can be resolved.

The generated [evidence policy](../scripts/workflow-template/evidence-policy.md)
supplies a shared positive inference process. It separates claims, combines
independent constraints, scopes negative evidence, permits justified corrections on
unchanged data and requires a compact rejection review before terminal acceptance.
The coordinator checks that reasoning rather than only its status. Valid completed
reviews are reusable; the policy retains an honest incomplete stop.

Investigation now lives entirely in redirect, at every classification percentage.
The former separate final-investigation phase and its percentage/byte thresholds
are retired. Redirect carries the same residual-ledger, static-structure, selector,
bounded-experiment and method-review work continuously. Supported proposals pass
through disassembly and commentary; useful intermediate findings or concrete next
methods continue redirect without an empty classification cycle. Historical attempts
remain evidence and unfinished legacy research resumes in redirect. Final editorial
acceptance and exact rebuilding remain separate completion gates.

It applies equally to Game Boy and SNES. Processor-specific context remains in the
platform guidance; no new runtime, bank-width or selector requirement is imposed on
Game Boy. Whole-ROM classification, editorial and exact-rebuild gates remain intact.

## Validation and limits

The workspace preparation regression checks that the policy is generated, unchanged
by either platform profile, and referenced by the coordinator and relevant phases.
It checks the positive inference instructions alongside safeguards against arbitrary
threshold fitting, unsupported extent expansion and endless retry loops. Existing
synthetic workflow and rebuild regressions check that preparation and fidelity still
work. They do not evaluate a live agent's scientific judgment.

A subsequent agent run should be assessed on reasoning and counterevidence, not
only byte gain. Useful behavioral cases include:

| Case | Expected judgment |
|---|---|
| Consumer-anchored quantized table fails one guessed exact rounding algorithm | Separate algorithm recovery from role/extent; justify any representation tolerance independently and compare classified and unknown cells consistently. |
| Mixed gap has an uncertain tag followed by a uniform bank suffix | Evaluate separate intervals and combined fill evidence; neither reject the suffix automatically nor classify it from uniformity alone. |
| Callerless candidate has a supported finite routine and calling contract | Assess static structure separately from actual arrival; do not invent bank associations or accept decoding alone. |
| Plausible pointers or a return opcode have no other structure | Retain uncertainty; relabeling a hypothesis does not supply evidence. |
| A prior exact-boundary counterexample genuinely defeats a candidate | Preserve that result; stop if rejection review supplies no useful changed inference or test. |

Changes to existing workspaces require updating their instruction files explicitly;
preparation does not rewrite them or restart agents. Historical investigation
artifacts remain unchanged. A resumed agent must read the updated policy before
relying on its previous terminal decision.
