# Exact stage-bank continuation

Claim: physical 116237..116260, CPU C5FD..C614, is Static Code. C5F7 decrements the stage byte, calls C614, increments it back, copies three palette bytes and restores the remembered bank. C614 calls F74A/F74D. Five LSRs of the selected stage byte leave a three-bit register selector 0..7; under mode 3 the exact possible lower pairs are [0,1], [2,3], ... [14,15]. Bit 4 chooses source CPU high 80/A0 independently, then C614 ORs high with 15 and sets low 30. It returns normally with the selected lower pair, not the incoming pair.

The nine instructions C5FD..C611 are therefore decoded under all eight possible effective pairs, with fixed upper [14,15]. They increment the stage back, set low pointer 5F, and loop Y=3,2,1, reading three source bytes into 04DE..04DC. The mapper remains unchanged until C60E calls F777. The final JMP at C611 executes after F777 restores the remembered incoming pair; its proposed views are the two supported enclosing routine contracts [12,13,14,15] and [10,11,14,15]. No old-bank preservation is asserted for C614/F74A themselves. The three-byte final JMP is decoded independently and physically identical under both restored contexts.

Primary evidence: stage-bank-local-primary.json; exact caller and helper source instructions in graph-final.json and commentary/source/game.asm. This finite bank-domain proof is independent of any natural round selection or observed tuple. It does not establish a general caller's remembered-bank invariant. The source palette bytes are already within previously classified noncode banks; this claim adds only the fixed continuation Code.
