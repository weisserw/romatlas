## Round 2 commentary pass (2026-11-21)

### How the source is produced
- `source/game.asm` is generated by `tools/build.py` from the frozen `before/game.asm`
  plus `tools/notes.txt` (round-1 annotations, carried forward by bank:CPU address and
  physical offset) and `tools/notes2.txt` (round-2 additions; loaded second, so its
  entries replace round-1 entries for the same key - a later header/note with its own
  comment lines replaces the earlier text instead of being appended).
- build.py also writes per-region notes for Data/Padding/Image regions that have no
  hand-written note, chosen from the region's phase-1 evidence (DPCM, sound data,
  screen chain, string framing, consumer-read tables - naming the reading routines -,
  trailers, vectors).  Hand-written `r`/`d` notes take precedence.
- Helpers: `tools/view.py NAME` (compact routine listing with addresses),
  `tools/where.sh LABEL`, `tools/thin.py` (routines lacking in-routine comments),
  `tools/addrlines.json`/`labels.json` (generated by build.py).
- Edit cycle: notes2.txt -> `python3 tools/build.py` -> `python3 tools/review.py` ->
  `commentary.py verify` -> `commentary.py read`.
