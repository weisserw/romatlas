## How the source is produced
commentary/source/game.asm is generated by commentary/tools/build.py from
before/game.asm plus commentary/tools/notes.txt. notes.txt holds the symbols,
routine names and headers, comments, data labels and gap notes. The build only adds
names and comments; every symbol has the same value as the number it replaces. To
edit, change notes.txt, then run build.py and review.py, then verify and read. See
commentary/progress.md.

## Readability assessment
- No address-only routine names remain. Every routine has a header. Branch labels use
  `Routine_Loop/_Skip/_Return`, with hand-chosen names in the key routines.
- Symbols:
  - hardware and MMC1 ports;
  - about 150 RAM variables, with bank-scoped names where zero page is reused;
  - ball-record fields (BALL_*) in operands;
  - constants for modes, player flags, buttons, fades, demo modes and PRG banks.
- Far calls are symbolic (`#<Far_X`, `PRG_*`). Pointer immediates for strings, screens,
  palettes and sprite grids are labelled. Text regions show their decoded text.
- Block comments are concentrated in MainLoop, NMI, the shot/rules code, the
  trial-shot simulation and ball movement. Smaller routines have header-level
  explanations only.

## Examples
- Bank 14 library:
  - FarCall ($C0AE/$D00C) maps PRG bank FarCallBank and calls FarCallTarget with A=FarCallArg.
  - NmiHandler ($C571) uploads the palette, metatile queue and text, then runs the sound tick and joypad read.
  - DecompressScreen ($CEB3) is an LZ decoder with a 64-byte window.
  - PrintString is a typewriter text printer.
  - There are also multiply, divide and square-root routines.
- MainLoop ($C1A7) runs title, menus, tour map, lag, rack, shot loop and match result.
- Bank 6/7: SimulateBalls, MoveBallBySpeed, CheckCushions, CollideBalls and the CPU shot
  planner. The planner uses CheckPath, TryCushions* and SimulateShotTrial, which
  test-fires each planned shot on a backup of the ball table.
- Bank 0: PlayVoiceSample switches MMC1 to control $1B and calls the DPCM player in
  banks 2-5.

## Corrections to phase-1 notes
