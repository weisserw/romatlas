- **Objective sufficiency.** Claim: the original candidate tank death should have
  ended the campaign. [Original setup](../../round1/flag-consistent-setup-route.json)
  used center83. [AF13 decode](graph-decode.json) independently requires saved code
  masked42 equal40/42 plus death80. [Exact corrected test](terminal-route.json)
  keeps16 result bytes unchanged and reaches defeat. The old result rules out that
  artificial route's sufficiency, not tank identity. Competing explanations such as
  turn-boundary-only evaluation are unnecessary for this counterexample; no fresh
  fight is required. Natural campaign victory remains a stronger unproved claim.
- **Final schema.** [Prior proof](../../round1/stage-data-proof.json) falsifies a
  universal exclusive3N index partition at selectors14/15. [Directory/windows](bank5-structure.json),

  combine independently. They support non-code and localized overlap/two-block
  structure, not a claim that all later third-slab reads are impossible. Generic
  non-code does not depend on recovering the full original schema.
- **Factory selection.** Early[trials](factory-menu-route.json) resumed inside a
  blocking chooser, and even the cache trial did not fetch the sampler. Its wrong
  HI/LO version is an implementation error, not game evidence. Actual[root sets](observed-union-summary.json)

  produced code20 and the panel. No PPU/toolkit defect follows. Early A before
  polling is not commit-negative;[confirmed commit](factory-commit-confirm-route.json)
  and[queue completion](factory-finish-route.json) are distinct.

- **Footer boundary.** Physical1FFEC..1FFEF is the supported JSR8033 after literal

  but the inspected8033 outcomes jump reset, so a return fetch is not established.
  Footer suffix spaces/CONFLICT is independent non-code evidence; it does not settle
  the leading byte alone. No automatic map change is proposed for it. Independent
  disassembly should inspect the exact stack/nonreturn contract and adjacent footer
  boundary; an unchanged gameplay replay is unlikely to resolve an unreachable tail.

  C01B/C000/C01B. The first mirror hypothesis was too broad. Bank3 footer is proposed
  only as generic non-code bounded by the FF suffix and physical bank end, with
  its reset-shaped word reported rather than an active-vector claim.
