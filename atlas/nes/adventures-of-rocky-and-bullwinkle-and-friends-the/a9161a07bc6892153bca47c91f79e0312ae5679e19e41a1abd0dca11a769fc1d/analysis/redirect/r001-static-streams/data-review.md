# Structural data review and limits

All claims are Static, ROM-local and proposed only. Generic data is sufficient where a finer schema or natural consumer is unresolved. The proposal range list is not a claim that exporter gaps are objects. `generic-review.json` saves the exact immutable bytes of every fallback slice and its inspected format group; `package-audit.json` checks these against all current and proposed instruction bytes.

Scene graphics: the eleven descriptor pointers select pair1..5. Encoded tile counts total3360. Every indexed pointer carries a polarity bit and relative address; decoding through the independently known 26-tile consumer emits exactly26 on every column without overrun. Sorting distinct encoded slices gives exact adjacency from end of the index to the independently descriptor-selected attribute start. Attribute header count equals width/4 for all11 scenes; index origins, payload starts spaced8, and palette pointer independently bound the storage. Seven bytes are copied; the eighth is retained as data without an invented field/fill meaning. Palettes copy16 bytes. No original compressor, natural selection, full game semantics or observed read is required.

Events: `events.json` is the preserved initially wrong whole-world extrapolation for the two temporary one-page roots. Its garbage pointers are counterexamples to that extent, not to the later final tables. The four initial tables each contain one pointer; their record chains replace8E56->8E69->8E48 and904F->9069->903B. `initial-events.json` and `events-revised.json` give exact records, payloads andFF terminals. Other final tables use width/32 world pages. Opcode0..4 record header is5 bytes, opcode5 is6; the last header byte supplies payload length. Event code and payloads are separate. Final-page event writes0783=FF and independently supports the ending test.

Terrain: walls select pair6 and read tag0 plus3 fields, terminating onFF. Surfaces are upper fixed-bank four-byte geometry records endingFF. Platforms are triples, with no sentinel: distinct pointer and next independent table anchors bound lists, and the full-X high-Y final row provides mechanical consistency. The extra pointer/triple1E0DB..1E0E0 is not folded into the preceding platform list. Final four D0,00,FF triples end at independent code1E1A6. The D669 helper clips its height difference to0..63, doubles it and readsD79D/D79E; the resulting exact64-word table physically1D7AD..1D82D meets the independent InstallEntityDescriptor entry. No exact quantized generator is asserted.

ConsumerC2B2 copies width*height tiles plusceil(width/4)*ceil(height/4) attributes after the6-byte header; most headers form an exact adjacent chain. The rest of this bank was inspected against12-byte descriptors, count-bounded frame pointer arrays, count-prefixed4-byte sprite records, and4-byte palette rows. The remaining generic slices contain continuing pointer/sprite/descriptor/palette patterns, not ambiguous instruction-shaped islands. Some lack a selected descriptor; selection is not required for non-code. No data-as-code conflict exists. Natural CHR ownership remains a semantic question.

DescriptorA55D independently fits the12-byte series, has type8E and points valid count/frames/palette, supporting AA30's dispatcher entry contract even though its natural initializer is unresolved.

The byte content and adjacent parsed structures support non-code, without treating missing callers as a barrier.
