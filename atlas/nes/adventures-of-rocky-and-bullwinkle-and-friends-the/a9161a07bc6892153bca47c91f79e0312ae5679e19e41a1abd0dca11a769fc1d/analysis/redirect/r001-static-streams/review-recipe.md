Authoritative payloads:

* routine-contracts.json: separately argued small routine bodies, stack/calling preconditions, reachability uncertainty and competing interpretation. Review each independently; no observed caller is required for an independently supported routine contract, and legal decoding alone is not the stated evidence.
* proposal-counts-final.json / package-audit.json / unknown-ledger.json: exact proposal difference, no accepted changes, overlap/length checks and all138 current unknown ranges split into dispositions.

Bank/return details: pair0 helpers C221/C1EA/C1F3/C203, arithmetic/collision/damage and sprite routines do not alter PRG. Sound/palette/screen wrappers save/restore SavedPrgPair; for a bank-preserving contract require Saved=Current at entry. Source-supported pair0 callers satisfy it. Pair4 audio static continuations may use Saved=Current=4 as the stated structural precondition; ordinary NMI execution with Saved0 is a different claim. InstallEntityDescriptor/DrawEntities restore pair0 through D9E0, so preservation is claimed only for pair0 callers, not arbitrary pairs. C198/C19B change lower slots and remain barriers. Separate roots assert pair6 after known literal6 selects and pair4 after literal4 selects. IRQ/NMI callbacks use Mmc3ModeShadow=0 from initialized reset state and select CHR registers0/1; their MMC3 writes do not change PRG, while timer/IRQ/mirroring writes likewise preserve PRG. The mapper mode invariant is a precondition, not an unbounded claim for arbitrary mutated hardware state.

ApplyForcedDirection, CheckSpecialSurfaceGeometry and event transitions have nonlocal stack behavior. The walkers stop at unresolved calls rather than globally assuming ordinary return; rooted ordinary branches and independently existing continuations are reviewed separately. The standalone3327 JMP remains outside all proposals.
