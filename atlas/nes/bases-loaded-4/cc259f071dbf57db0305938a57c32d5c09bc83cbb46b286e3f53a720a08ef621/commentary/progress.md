- Generic data headers: pass-002/L014 provenance, a per-unit area note (DATA_AREAS) and the
  nearest named table within 256 bytes.

  U28_SineTable, "points at data, not code").

  stored to a zero-page pointer (38 sites); sublabels are used only within 128 bytes.

  their first action (@CallPlaySound, @GoToX, @SetVar, @TestVar). Generic labels went from
  1,782 to 1,222 (760 @Skip_, 294 @Loop_, 168 @Return_).
