The handler returns to FFC2 PLA/TAY; FFC4 mapper write restores saved physical bank before FFC7 STA03/FFC9 RTS. Thus RAM00 calls are special reviewed indirect service contracts, not unexplained ordinary ROM calls. Bank1 front directory is nine pairs, bank2 fourteen, bank3 ten, ending at independently established first routines. B0 B165 removes BRK interrupt status, subtracts two from stacked PC, and tail-enters FFA2; final RTS resumes BRK+1. No inline selector byte. Other banks' RTI vectors retain their own contract.

All absolute cartridge stores in graph are common-tail switch/restore/reset plus B0 A203/A207. The latter write via ROM bytes 10/00 at A20B/C after EOR10 on saved mapper state: lower bank bits stay unchanged under B0 entry contract. Indexed stores in ordinary paths are bounded zero-page/stack RAM or hardware addresses, not unspecified cartridge writes. Stack manipulations preserve temporary A/Y/loop state in local paths; special interrupt/BRK stack transforms are separately handled above. The returning-path audit supports structural continuation under the documented preconditions, not universal termination or natural caller selection.
