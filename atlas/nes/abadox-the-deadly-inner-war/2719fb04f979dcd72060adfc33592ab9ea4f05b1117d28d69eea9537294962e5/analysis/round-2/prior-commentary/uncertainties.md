# Round 1 semantic limits

Current-source readability is complete; these are explicit interpretation limits, not unreviewed ranges. Unknown ROM spans retain their original physical boundaries and classification. The supported mechanical interpretation is explained next to each routine in source/game.asm.

- Stage scripts in physical bank 3 dispatch return-address pairs, gate on coarse scroll and write timed CHR selectors. Their exact visual scene/animation names and all natural selector states remain unresolved. StageChrSequenceA/B, StageScrollStateAdvance and StageChrPaletteSequence name the mechanical roles.
- Ending/credits reuse gameplay RAM. Credits scroll and row-address aliases are local to the bank-10 contract; ending ship coordinates are distinct aliases for the primary-object area. Timeline phase bodies in unknown ROM are not invented. The final spacecraft exit is conditional on shortcutStage; ordinary sequential ending reachability was not established by this commentary pass.
- Packed terrain consumes two-bit cells and returns zero, one or $80 under a stage mode. Precise material names are unresolved. Source describes the coordinate transform, extraction and response tests without assigning terrain art names.
- Player weapon/option/attachment levels have established allocation, timing, collision and drawing mechanics; original upgrade names remain tentative. Player state numeric values describe dispatch behavior rather than an invented complete animation taxonomy.
- Object kinds/extended script commands whose bodies remain unknown retain numeric selectors and raw external call targets. Movement and animation ordinary records, 21-byte primary records, six-byte player-shot records and nine-byte secondary records are described fully where classified. Referenced unknown command bodies are not counted as decoded.
- Four initialized bytes probably seed random state; their consumers are outside the classified source. No specific generator is claimed. The sound request scratch byte aliases a timer-array cell under another logical track context; it is not presented as an independent persistent counter.
- Sound mechanics are recovered at the interpreter level: six logical tracks, priority, raw/melodic events, one saved substream return, loops, pitch/volume envelopes, release, period history and DMC configuration. Exact song/effect names, untested sound IDs and complete descriptor/envelope table bounds remain unresolved. Array names refer to first-slot addresses; logical track indexing can alias separately named scratch.
- Seven first-bank metasprite groups have bounded pointer/record partitions. Runtime selection coverage and all consumers are unresolved. Uniform data runs remain honestly generic: their original fill/allocation purpose is unknown. CHR backing is tile image storage; individual art identities and complete bank-selector coverage are unresolved.
