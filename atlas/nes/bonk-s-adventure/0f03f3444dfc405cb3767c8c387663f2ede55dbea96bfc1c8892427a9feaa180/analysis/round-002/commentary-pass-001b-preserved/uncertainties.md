- The stage selector at RAM $0459 indexes layout-bank, CHR, layout-index, record, metatile, dimension, flag, palette and sound tables (source/game.asm, P02_LoadStageTables and adjacent entries, physical 20604..20732). The fixed-bank scene initializer loads stage $37 in the classified scrolling sequence. Table contents/backing that remain phase-1 Unknown have not been relabeled. Directly writing stage RAM alone is not yet a proven complete transition contract.
- Inline object-state dispatch pops a JSR return pointer, advances to the inline table and dispatches by state minus one (P30_DispatchInlineObjectState, within physical 255045..255173). Object-type/player dispatch tables are similarly explicit word consumers. Enumerate bounded target domains from established states/call sites; pointers alone do not prove executable targets, and mapper calls can change the effective view.
- Camera-edge spawning reads four-byte records, with sentinel comparisons, X-bound search, duplicate history checks and five nonplayer slots (P30_SpawnAtHorizontalCameraEdge, physical 253727..254261). Visible mechanics support a record-consumer hypothesis; the complete record bounds and stage-specific pointer origins remain to establish. Vertical spawning enters a largely unknown path.
- Five-byte metatile records supply packed palette/material bits and four tile IDs. Horizontal layout records use stride 15; vertical records stride 16 (fixed-bank strip decoders). The PRG banks 22..29 patterned data may contain related structures, but no specific association is asserted without matching the stage's effective bank/pointer and bounded indices. Prior full pattern/neighbor inspection is preserved in analysis/round-001/pattern-data-inspection.txt and uniform-data-inspection.json.
- Player state IDs, overlay effects, status modes, material interactions and many sound identities have only mechanical interpretations. Names describe the observed state changes, counters and flags. Unresolved RAM aliases keep CPU addresses; overlapping scratch has no single game meaning.
- Audio mechanics are identifiable (five logical channels, four hardware APU channel windows, dirty shadows, note/gate timing, four-deep counted loops, envelope/pitch scripts). Song/command identities and remaining stream/table bounds are unresolved. The code's DMC-shadow handling does not establish full DMC playback compatibility.
- Reset/NMI vectors have exact aliases; IRQ vector $C281 and many literal call destinations remain structurally unknown in the current map. Their surrounding binary bytes retain honest uncertainty. No full-game reachability, whole-ROM completion or hardware compatibility claim follows from this phase.
