Structural classification and reachability are separate claims. Require evidence
for the claimed structural role, exact boundaries and effective bank context;
require reachability evidence only when claiming reachability. A live caller,
observed instruction fetch, naturally produced selector or ordinary gameplay route
is useful evidence, not a universal prerequisite for classifying code or data.
Apply this distinction from the first disassembly pass and throughout redirect
investigation. Independently supported routine contracts, control/data flow and
bounded record/table formats can justify static classification while natural use
remains unresolved. Record structural evidence and reachability status separately;
do not label statically inferred bytes Observed or claim they are unused/dead
merely because no caller is known.

Before falling back to generic data, make a practical attempt to identify its role:
inspect available references and consumers, nearby code and labels, and obvious
formats or repeated record layouts. Use supported specific region types, meaningful
labels and concise format/role explanations when the evidence permits. Generic data
is a fallback after this bounded inspection, not the default shortcut. Record briefly
what was checked and what remains unresolved; reuse prior sound inspection rather
than repeating it. Follow concrete promising leads, but do not require exhaustive
semantic research or block classification when further investigation is unproductive.
Never invent a specific meaning or label just to avoid the generic fallback.

Caller/selector tracing remains useful for resolving roles, bounds and bank context.
The primary classification task is separating code from data. Anything reasonably
identified as non-code may be an unlabeled generic data block; its format, consumer,
precise table layout and game meaning need not be understood. Clear repeated byte
patterns, uniform runs and visibly non-instruction content can justify generic data
classification after inspecting boundaries and checking for conflicting code evidence.
Record the physical range, brief rationale and Static provenance through map.annotate;
count these bytes as classified and complete. No semantic label is required.
Do not leave such bytes unknown or report blocked merely because their meaning,
references or original organization cannot be recovered. Revisit a data block if
later code evidence contradicts it. Lack of execution alone does not prove data;
retain unknown only where the code-versus-data decision or physical bounds remain
materially ambiguous. Raw export fallback alone still does not classify bytes.

When selected indices are the only evidence for a table's extent, an
independent extent proof is needed to claim that specific table layout, not to classify surrounding non-code
bytes as generic data. Valid decoding and pointer values alone do not prove code;
pattern evidence for generic data need not establish a specific structure.
Artificial execution can test consumer mechanics but does not establish
intended code, complete table extent or natural reachability by itself. Keep these
evidence safeguards and all classification, fidelity and editorial completion gates.

Run the redirect phase after the current-source commentary handoff gate is met.
Incomplete ROM classification is allowed; interrupted commentary must resume first.
Read evidence-policy.md. Start from the compact commentary handoff and analysis/leads.json. Retrieve relevant
source, uncertainties, review records, state and investigation evidence as needed.
Accept commentary on an incompletely classified ROM; do not wait for final archive
audit, but require completion of the current-source readability scope.
Search the available commented source for all identifiable leads in this pass:
missing callees, dispatch paths, table consumers, bank selection, unresolved
pointers, state-dependent references and unexplained structures. Prefer static
analysis and concrete missing pieces over speculative gameplay exploration.
Also inspect uncommented sections, generic/generated labels and commentary's
explicit unreviewed gaps directly; do not assume missing comments mean there are
no leads. Use observable-behavior notes and uncertainty questions as starting points,
confirming them against source and evidence rather than trusting review status alone.

Every redirect pass should investigate promising unresolved questions thoroughly
but reasonably. Do the groundwork needed to turn a credible question into an
answer or actionable next step; do not stop at an inventory of uncertainties.
Follow relevant callers, writers, consumers, bank contexts and downstream tables
as far as they continue to resolve uncertainty or improve a concrete experiment.
Small supported findings and useful intermediate understanding count as progress;
no immediate substantial byte gain or complete global selector domain is required.
There is no fixed hypothesis count, experiment quota or one-time research allowance.


Investigate connected behavior as a coherent batch. Follow a productive dispatcher
through selected handlers, state transitions, shared helpers and associated tables
or assets while each continuation has concrete support and a useful prospect.
Finishing the originally supplied range, producing the first ready lead, or reaching
a handler entry is not by itself a reason to hand off. Inspect that handler and its
supported continuations in this redirect rather than automatically scheduling each
successive piece for another full disassembly/commentary/redirect cycle.
Use bank-aware static decoding and control/data-flow reasoning for ordinary paths;
actual instruction fetches are not required for every code classification proposal.
Keep static inference distinct from observed execution and validate exact boundaries,
inline-data barriers and effective bank context. Use bounded runtime experiments to
resolve specific uncertainties, not to demand execution of every branch. Reached
addresses and pointer values alone still do not prove code or execution.

End a connected investigation when evidence runs out, a specific missing prerequisite
blocks useful continuation, results repeat, or prospects diminish. Do not demand
recursive closure of every handler/state or a complete global selector domain.
No minimum byte quota or exhaustive per-range review is required. In the handoff,
identify the obvious supported continuations and explain why any are deferred:
missing fact, failed method, diminishing prospects, or interruption. If productive
work is interrupted, save its exact frontier and resume redirect; do not call the
research exhausted. Package supported findings together with reusable evidence and
recipes so disassembly can validate/classify the batch without replaying unchanged
experiments or splitting known connected pieces into artificial extra rounds.


Completing one connected investigation is a checkpoint, not a phase stop condition.
After closing a graph or packaging ready findings, assess other already-identified
promising avenues using the evidence now available. Continue with actionable
independent avenues in this same redirect, accumulating a combined proposal for
one disassembly/commentary cycle. An avenue being independent, another actor type,
or outside the first selected graph is not itself a reason to defer it. For example,
accepted initializers that produce states with unknown handlers warrant inspecting
those handlers and their prerequisites; do not arbitrarily select only two actors
and reserve the others for future rounds.

Do not exhaustively enumerate every actor or unknown range. Triage concrete existing
opportunities; follow those with a credible method and useful prospects, and stop
ones requiring unsupported assumptions or yielding repetitive results. End redirect
when no identified promising actionable avenue remains under this standard, not
merely when one graph closes or a small batch is ready. For each obvious deferred
avenue, give its actual missing prerequisite, negative evidence or diminished
prospect; 'independent investigation' or 'outside selected scope' is insufficient.
Context pressure calls for a compact research checkpoint and a continuation of the
same redirect phase, not a forced full workflow iteration. This is no byte quota
and does not require exhaustive game completion or proof for every unknown range.

Prioritize avenues with direct evidence and a credible connection to unknown ROM
structure or behavior. State the question, missing fact, evidence, planned method
and what result would change the next step. Revise the method as findings warrant.
Do not chase rabbit holes, pursue slim chances, brute-force arbitrary states or
keep expanding scope merely because more questions exist. Stop an avenue when
results become repetitive, assumptions accumulate without support, or further work
has little credible prospect of useful progress. Switch to a promising independent
avenue when available; end the pass when reasonable avenues are exhausted.
A prior failed attempt may be reopened only with new evidence or a materially
different justified method. Preserve the original outcome and explain the change.
Reassess the reasoning as well as the experiment: evidence-policy.md permits a
corrected premise, localized counterexample or newly combined independent constraints
on unchanged evidence. Retrieve the decisive artifact before inheriting a rejection.
Record findings as work proceeds, including negative results and interruption state,
so later redirects can build on evidence rather than repeat the same search.

Use run.py serve and docs/protocol.md for bounded hypothesis tests when helpful.
Inspect api.describe. Use emulator.memory.write and emulator.registers.set to
advance levels, positions and progression flags or expose needed pointers/tables,
saving/restoring snapshots and
recording exact patches, effective banks, inputs and budgets. ROM backing remains
immutable. Debugger mutations prove no execution or natural reachability; clearly
separate artificial-state observations from ordinary gameplay evidence. Keep each
runtime call bounded by the documented API limits; choose budgets appropriate to
the question. Reassert intended buttons after restoration when required by the
toolkit. These per-call safety limits do not impose an overall research quota.

Write the persistent leads file analysis/leads.json (version 1, passes and leads
arrays). Each lead needs a stable id, physical ranges and CPU/bank context, source
locations, hypothesis, evidence/artifact links, a concrete bounded verification
recipe, expected useful gain (or the specific missing fact and downstream
opportunity when gain is not yet measurable), priority, status and attempt history. Preserve
all previous entries and outcomes. Match by target/hypothesis, not wording.
Do not reissue a previous lead without additional evidence or an improved actionable
recipe; append the new information and explain why it reopens the lead. Mark new
or materially improved actionable leads ready; retain rejected/blocked/verified
history. Record every pass's input checkpoint and added/updated IDs, including an
empty result. Summarize unresolved gaps and why remaining ideas are not actionable.
Do not classify candidates just to create leads. Do not repeat stalled work internally or attempt
exhaustive game completion. Return the lead list for the next disassembly pass.

Return ready leads when evidence and a concrete recipe provide a useful next step,
even if the expected byte gain is small or not yet measurable. An unanswered
question alone does not justify another round. If none remain after reasonable
investigation, report incomplete and explain the remaining obstacles. Research
findings belong in durable evidence/lead records; leave classification changes to
the disassembly phase. Preserve all map, fidelity and completion requirements.

Readable, fully commented and labeled assembly is a required final deliverable for
porting, separate from structural classification. Commentary must explain meaningful
routines, branches and data structures and use consistent variable/field/constant
symbols. Each round completes the current readability backlog; final acceptance
addresses remaining naming/comment gaps even in previously reviewed ranges. Unknown
meanings must have useful mechanical descriptions and explicit uncertainty.

Investigation continues at every classification percentage. A ready redirect lead
is not required: design concrete alternative methods from residual barriers using
the latest handoffs and accepted/rejected outcomes. Test independent whole-table
extent proofs, ending source/bank/copy-length contracts, graphics-container bounds
and other supported alternatives to missing callers/selectors as ordinary redirect
work. These are hypotheses, not preset classification results.

Maintain the exact unknown complement from the internal map with current counts,
bank/CPU context, neighbors and evidence-linked dispositions grouped by structure.
Refresh it after map changes and before terminal assessment; reuse unchanged ledger
entries rather than repeating a whole-ROM survey. Every remaining range receives
a disposition, not an exhaustive experiment. Ledger groups are bookkeeping, not
indivisible candidate objects; split claims when a missing fact or counterexample
affects only part. Do not infer structure from exporter raw gaps.

Audit boundaries, record strides, terminators, encodings, consumer semantics and
bank-aware control/stack contracts. Compare independent constraints with known
structures using evidence-policy.md. Trace missing selectors backward through
writers, initialization, transformations, gates and bank changes. Separate possible
and observed values; an excluded selector does not prove an apparent target unused.
Targeted artificial experiments must discriminate a specific hypothesis; arbitrary
successful execution establishes neither structure nor natural reachability.

Keep new research in a unique attempt directory analysis/redirect/<attempt-id>/.
Preserve completed handoffs, ledgers and evidence; never overwrite or discard prior
attempts, including legacy analysis/final-investigation/ records. Resume an unfinished
legacy investigation here from its saved frontier. Before a substantive retry record
the target claim, prior barrier/method/outcome, changed fact or justified inference,
expected discriminating result and stop condition. Compare decisive artifacts, not
only prior method labels. No unchanged replay, arbitrary state sweep or fixed pass quota.

Supported proposals return to disassembly for independent review, then full
current-source commentary and redirect. Useful intermediate evidence or a credible
improved method without a proposal continues redirect directly, without an empty
disassembly/commentary cycle. Record the frontier and continue across context
rollover; do not call an interruption or completed attempt terminal exhaustion.

Before a terminal incomplete handoff, include the compact rejection review required
by evidence-policy.md for coordinator inspection. A missing-fact list or prior worker's
exhaustion summary alone does not establish it. Preserve ROM, map, state, source and
configured binary; redirect changes research/lead records only. State checkpoint
identity, proposed versus accepted bytes, exact residuals, methods/results, useful
constraints, concrete next recipes or actual exhaustion reasons in the handoff.
If no useful revised inference or test survives review, report incomplete. When all
bytes later become classified, final editorial audit and verification remain required.

## Broad gameplay progression

Basic gameplay means broad progression through the game, not just startup, menus
or the first encounter. Make a deliberate effort to visit later levels/areas,
distinct gameplay mechanics, bosses, major transitions and the ending/credits or
other terminal screens where present. Discovering these states is useful even
before a specific unknown ROM range or measurable byte gain is identified.
Avoid exhaustive permutations of collisions, rare interactions or every possible
state; representative visits to distinct systems and progression milestones suffice.

Use emulator.memory.write proactively to bypass slow or difficult progression:
identify level/stage, position, checkpoint, health/lives, inventory/key or progression
flags from source, watches and bounded experiments, then patch suitable RAM values.
Prefer a supported stage/checkpoint transition so dependent state is initialized;
use emulator.registers.set only with an understood entry contract. Do not require a
preexisting classification lead or proof that normal button inputs can reach a state
before trying this. Keep ROM backing unchanged. Save a clean snapshot before edits,
record exact addresses/values, bank context, inputs and budgets, and restore before
trying an alternative. Inspect state/frame output and actual execution after the
patch; a written stage number alone does not demonstrate a successful level visit.
Exercise representative mechanics and transitions in each reached state with bounded
inputs rather than merely displaying it. Actual accepted instruction fetches after
patching establish observed execution in that artificial scenario, not natural
reachability; edits themselves and reached targets prove neither execution nor code.

Maintain a progression checklist in analysis/scenarios.json with milestone, route
or patch recipe, observed result, evidence links and status (visited, attempted,
unavailable or deferred with reason). Reuse successful snapshots/routes across rounds.
Before declaring gameplay exhausted or handing off terminally, review unvisited
later-game and ending milestones and attempt a practical shortcut where possible.
Early-game repetition or difficult manual play alone is not grounds to stop while
an obvious state edit or supported shortcut remains. If the necessary RAM contract
is unknown, investigate it in redirect rather than retrying long button sequences.
Do not brute-force arbitrary RAM or spend indefinitely forcing a broken state;
record specific obstacles and try another representative milestone. Aim to reach
the ending, but documented unsuccessful attempts or unavailable transitions do not
require endless experimentation. Preserve classification, fidelity and readability
gates; this is broad best-effort exploration, not an exhaustive gameplay/fidelity gate.
